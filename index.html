How it works

An AI lead agent that gets sharper the more you use it

Connect a Google Sheet of jobs you've quoted. HandyMiner reads every job you've logged — the wins and the no-gos — and goes hunting for more leads that look like your best customers while quietly tuning out the ones that don't, by trade, by ticket size, by neighborhood, by whatever pattern it sees in your sheet.

Mines real leads

Pulls from public business listings, permit data, and review feeds. Filters them through your trade, your service area, and the patterns it sees across every job you've logged.

Learns from every job

Mark a job won, lost, or no-show — whatever happened, HandyMiner notices. Wins teach it who to target; losses teach it who to skip. The next batch shifts accordingly.

Drops them in your sheet

No new dashboard to learn. New leads land in the same Google Sheet you already use to track jobs. Quote, dispatch, mark the outcome — that's the whole loop.
